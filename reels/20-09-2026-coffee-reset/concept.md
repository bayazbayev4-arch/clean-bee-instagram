Only the *camera-move-hides-the-transformation* device is reused — no shots, audio, people or brands are copied from any of these.

**Format in one line:** the camera slowly orbits Bee once, unbroken; by the time it completes the arc back to his front, the coffee stain on his shirt is gone.

**Clothes moment:** an everyday coffee spill on a shirt — the exact kind of stain химчистка (live service) removes.

**Story, 3 beats:**
1. Bee at his kitchen counter, morning light, laughing on a call and gesturing with his coffee mug — the mug tips, coffee splashes onto his light shirt, a dark stain blooms.
2. He freezes, looks down at the stain. The camera begins a slow ~180° orbit around him, unbroken — no cut. Partway through the arc, out of direct view, the stain is gone; by the time the camera settles back in front of him the shirt reads crisp and clean.
3. Bee looks down, grins, relieved, and goes back to his call/day — calm, readable last beat before the end card.
