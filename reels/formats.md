# Fallback Reel formats

Used when no trend this week fits (daily-reel step 1). Each: the format → the clothes moment → an end-card line. Tie a line to a service only if that service has no «Скоро» on the prices page in that run.

1. **Slow-motion spill freeze** — liquid arcs out in near-frozen droplets and lands on a white shirt → «Пятно? Курьер заберёт у двери»
2. **Camera through an object** — the lens glides through a window, sunglasses or a glass door into a calm, fresh wardrobe → «Чистота на лету»
3. **Shoes-to-face one-take** — low dolly rising up a confident walk in a crisp suit on the way to an event → «Костюм к событию — без хлопот»
4. **Everything freezes but him** — the city stops mid-rain; he walks through hanging drops, coat dry and fresh → «Свежесть прямо к порогу»
5. **Car splash** — a passing car sprays slush across a light winter coat in slow motion → «Слякоть? Заберём пальто у двери»
6. **Macro fabric dive** — the camera flies into the weave of a shirt; dirt specks lift away into light → «Бережная химчистка»
7. **Bedding cloud** — the duvet lifts off the bed like a cloud, floats out the window and drifts back crisp → «Одеяло свежее, как облако»
8. **Time-lapse morning** — sunrise to evening in one shot: stain in the morning, clean shirt on the hanger by night → «Заберите время себе»
