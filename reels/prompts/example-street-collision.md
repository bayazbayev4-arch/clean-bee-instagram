# Reference prompt — street collision (Bee, 14-09-2026)

The house format for every Reel prompt: one continuous shot, named references (`@PERSON`, `@PROP`…) with "identical" locks, second-by-second beats covering all 10 s, a STYLE line and a sound line. It is also the fixed prompt for the A/B/C model test — run it unchanged on every model so only the model differs.

For the test: `@PERSON` = an AI still of Bee made from his saved face, `@GLASSES` = a generated pair of sunglasses, `@DRINK` = a generated can of pink lemonade with abstract label art and **no letters or brand**. The Clean Bee brand comes only from the end card.

## Prompt (verbatim)

A single continuous 10-second cinematic shot, summer afternoon, sunlit European city street with pastel neoclassical facades, wide empty road on the left, stone-paved sidewalk. SUBJECT: the person from @ PERSON — identical face, and full outfit preserved exactly. PROP A: he wears the sunglasses from @ GLASSES— identical frame shape, metal colour, lens tint. PROP B: he carries drink from @ DRINK — identical can, label artwork, and liquid colour. 0.0–2.0s — Extreme low angle, camera 15cm off the ground, dollying BACKWARD at walking pace, framed tight on his shoes striding toward lens on grey paving stones. Shallow depth of field. 2.0–4.5s — Without a cut, the camera continues retreating while craning upward and tilting up: pants,, torso, then his upper body and face. he is mid-call, phone at his ear, drink in the other hand, sunglasses on, walking straight at the lens with a confident unbothered expression. 4.5–5.5s — A man in a beige linen suit walks into frame from the right and their shoulders collide. His head snaps, momentum breaks. 5.5–7.5s — SLOW MOTION begins and deepens. The sunglasses lift off his face and tumble toward the lens; the can slips from his fingers and rotates in the air; the liquid spills out the top and a rope of pink liquid lemonade arcs out in suspended droplets. he is soft-focus behind it, arm still raised with the phone. 7.5–10.0s — EXTREME slow motion, near-frozen. The camera glides forward and passes THROUGH the tinted sunglasses lens — the world warms to amber for a beat as the frame rim sweeps past — then racks focus onto the tumbling can, ending on a macro hold on the label as lemonade droplets hang mid-air. STYLE: shot on 35mm anamorphic, warm golden grade, creamy bokeh, natural sunlight with hard shadows, high-speed camera realism for the slow-motion half, seamless single take, no cuts. Diegetic street ambience only.
