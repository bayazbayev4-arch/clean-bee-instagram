# Kazakh + English slides — translation guide

Used by `skills/daily-ig-post/SKILL.md` when `config.slide_languages` lists more than Russian (Bee, 21-09-2026: every feed post = carousel, Russian slide → Kazakh slide → English slide). Russian is always written first and is the source; the other slides say the same thing.

## Rules
- Translate the meaning, not word for word. Short, warm, everyday words — the same voice as `brand.md`.
- Same number of tips as the Russian slide, same order. Headline ≤ 7 words (Kazakh runs longer; compose.py shrinks the text to fit).
- Never change facts: numbers, prices, percentages, dates, times and the service list stay exactly as in Russian. Never add a claim the Russian doesn't make.
- Never translate: **Clean Bumble Bee**, **cleanbumblebee.com**, **Kaspi**. Astana = **Астана** (kk) / **Astana** (en).
- **Kazakh:** polite «Сіз» form (жуыңыз, беріңіз, қараңыз). Always the real Kazakh letters **Ә Ғ Қ Ң Ө Ұ Ү Һ І** — never the Russian look-alikes (к for қ, у for ұ/ү, н for ң, о for ө). Months: қаңтар, ақпан, наурыз, сәуір, мамыр, маусым, шілде, тамыз, қыркүйек, қазан, қараша, желтоқсан. Date style: «31 желтоқсанға дейін».
- **English:** British spelling, like the app (colour, trainers, jumper, trousers).
- Prefer the terms below — they are the app's and website's own Kazakh/English (from the app's catalogue and interface translations, 12-08-2026), so the posts match what customers see after they tap the link.
