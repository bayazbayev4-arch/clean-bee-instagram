## Voice (Russian)
- Talk like a helpful neighbour: short sentences, «вы», no bureaucratic words, no exclamation-mark spam (max one).
- Outcome first: time saved, no trips to the dry cleaner, clothes cared for.
- Emojis: 0–2 per caption. Hashtags: 5–8, local + topic (#астана #химчисткаастана #стиркаастана …).
- Every caption ends with one simple action: «Закажите в приложении Clean Bumble Bee — ссылка в профиле».

## How it works (claims must match this)
1. Pick services and a 2-hour pickup window.
2. Courier picks up at the door, codes each bag, photos the handover.
3. Station weighs and inspects; the exact price is sent as a **Kaspi invoice**. Cleaning starts only after it's paid.
4. Clean items come back in the chosen window. Status is tracked in the app.
- Every handover is photographed; different services never share a bag.
- App in Russian, Kazakh and English. Service area: Astana only.
